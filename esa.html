Department Overview

About the Association

The Electrical Engineering Association at NKOCET is committed to empowering students in electric power systems, microgrids, renewable energy storage, and industrial control. We bridge electromagnetic theory with industrial application through rigorous laboratory simulation and project competitions.

⚡ Power Systems Mastery

Develop expertise in generation, transmission, load-flow distribution analysis, and protection relays through practical simulations and real-time modeling.

🔋 Renewable Energy Focus

Promote sustainable energy solutions including commercial solar photovoltaic setups, wind generators, and battery energy storage systems (BESS).

🤖 Automation & Industrial Control

Master industrial PLC ladder logic, SCADA network integration, sensor feedback, and modern variable frequency drives (VFD).

💡 Smart Grid Technology

Explore bidirectional smart metering, modern grid synchrophasors, IoT in substation equipment, and dynamic demand-response management.

🏭 Industrial Plant Liason

Connect with thermal/hydro power generating stations, 400kV substations, and electrical switchgear industries through guided technical study tours.

🎓 EV & Mobility Research

Facilitate research into electric vehicle battery management systems (BMS), high-power inverter topologies, and rapid charging infrastructure.

Department Infrastructure

Specialized Electrical Laboratories

Advanced electrical machinery benches, digital power simulators, and renewable energy testing testbeds.

🔌

Electrical Machines Lab

  • DC Shunt, Series & Compound Machines
  • Single-Phase & 3-Phase Power Transformers
  • Squirrel-Cage & Slip-Ring Induction Motors
  • Salient-Pole Synchronous Alternators
  • Special Stepper & Servo Motor Test Benches
⚙️

Power Systems & Simulation Lab

  • ETAP & MATLAB Power Flow Simulators
  • Transmission Line Generalized ABCD Models
  • Overcurrent & Differential Protection Relays
  • HVDC Transmission Line Analog Models
  • Symmetrical & Unsymmetrical Fault Analyzers
🎛️

Control Systems & Automation Lab

  • Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC) Kits
  • Supervisory Control & Data Acquisition (SCADA)
  • PID Closed-Loop Temperature & Level Trainers
  • DC & AC Position Servomechanisms
  • Process Control Instrumentation Benches
⚡

Power Electronics & Drives Lab

  • Controlled SCR Bridge Rectifiers & Inverters
  • DC-DC Buck, Boost & Buck-Boost Converters
  • Sinusoidal PWM Pulse Gate Drivers
  • Switched Mode Power Supply (SMPS) Design Kits
  • Variable Frequency AC Motor Drive Controllers
📊

Electrical Measurements & Instrumentation

  • High-Precision Digital Power Analyzers
  • Digital Storage Oscilloscopes (DSO) with FFT
  • Arbitrary Function & Signal Generators
  • Class 0.2 Accurate Energy Measurement Benches
  • LCR Meters & Kelvin Double Bridges
☀️

Renewable Energy & Smart Grid Lab

  • Rooftop Solar PV Array Test Facility
  • Grid-Tied Micro-Inverter Integration Setup
  • Emulated Wind Turbine Generator Test Rig
  • Lithium Iron Phosphate Battery Test Racks
  • Smart Energy Monitoring Microgrid Node
