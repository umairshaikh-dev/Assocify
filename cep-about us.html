ABOUT ASSOCIATIONHUB

AssociationHub is a Student Association and Club Awareness Portal designed to make it easier for students to discover department associations, understand their activities, and explore opportunities to participate in campus life.

🎯

Our Mission

Help students discover their department associations and access reliable information about campus activities.

🔭

Our Vision

Create a connected campus community where students can discover opportunities, share ideas, and participate in meaningful experiences.

Academic Background

About the CEP Initiative

This website was conceptualized as a College Engagement Project (CEP) for 2nd Year, 3rd Semester B.Tech students. The core objective is to spread active awareness about collegiate department associations among incoming freshers, fostering greater participation in hackathons, seminars, and clubs right from day one.
