Department Overview

About the Association

The Mechanical Engineering Association at NKOCET bridges foundational physics with high-performance manufacturing. From hands-on metal fabrication to computational finite element analysis (FEA), MESA equips engineers for the industrial frontier.

🔧 Technical Excellence

Enhance practical engineering execution through intensive hands-on workshops on CAD/CAM modeling, 5-axis CNC machining, advanced welding, and metallurgy.

🏭 Industrial Exposure

Organize guided industrial visits to automobile assembly plants, heavy forging lines, and thermal power generation hubs for real-world manufacturing insight.

🚗 Competitive Vehicle Design

Support interdisciplinary student teams engineering off-road all-terrain BAJA buggies, formula student go-karts, and electric drive powertrains.

🎯 National Competition Mentorship

Prepare student cohorts for BAJA SAE, SUPRA SAE, E-BAJA, and national technical paper presentation stages with senior faculty guidance.

🌱 Sustainable Engineering

Promote energy-efficient design, eco-conscious material selection, waste-heat recovery mechanisms, and alternative renewable fuels.

📐 Precision CAD/CAE Modeling

Foster design thinking through 3D parametric modeling contests, structural stress simulations, and aerodynamic wind tunnel studies.

CAD / CAM / CAE Center

  • Dassault Systèmes SolidWorks Workstations
  • Autodesk AutoCAD Mechanical Drafting
  • CATIA Parametric Surfacing Suites
  • ANSYS Mechanical & CFD Simulation Engines
  • CAM Toolpath Generation Modules

Thermal & IC Engines Lab

  • Computerized Multi-Cylinder Engine Test Rigs
  • Vapor Compression Refrigeration Testbeds
  • Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger Calorimeters
  • Industrial Boiler & Steam Flow Cut-Sections
  • Air Conditioning & Psychrometric Setup

Fluid Mechanics & Machinery Lab

  • Subsonic Aerodynamic Wind Tunnel System
  • Pelton Wheel & Francis Turbine Test Setups

  • Venturi & Orifice Flow Measurement Calibration

Metrology & Quality Inspection Lab
